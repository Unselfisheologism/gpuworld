### Now, let's look at the different aspects of a human's lives, in such a future:

**1. Medicine AND healthcare:** Drug discovery, Disease curing research, Cell and RNA and Protein research, and similar fields, all ubiquitously turn into continuously running (24/7/365) research laboratories, with bare minimum human involvement. Heck, there might even be tailor-made medicines suited just for your condition. Vibe-drugs, i guess. Besides, your personal doctor might as well be living inside your 'head-band' device (or similar AI device), which will let your 'doctor' get real-time health data about your vitals, and diagnosis will be instant. Hospitals will work 24/7/365 and there won't be ANY wait for doctors or lack of doctors (currently a very huge issue in countries like USA and places like the EU ).

**2. Education:** Besides the obvious benefit of each of the 8 Billion people having their own expert mentor for each field of interest they want to study about, there is the added benefit of having most people to adopt kinaesthetic learning (learning through experience) and self-paced learning. Unless you've witnessed first-hand, the benefits of these learning methodologies aren't obvious to you. Especially learning through experience in my opinion, is the only method (out of all that i've tried) has made me innately passionate and curious about a subject. AI accelerating learning through experience (Plus, self-paced learning) is undoubtedly the biggest multiplier in educational gains.

**3. Economics:** People invest and trade with a certain belief in an organization or a group or an entity that has created a valuable (or deemed as valuable) asset, into which people can invest in or trade with. Why would this exist in the future? It won't. Why will people trust other people's created-assets, when more than half of the portion of their decisions are tied to and influenced by their 'EXTENSIONS' (AI) ? they won't have any trust. We might as well go back to the 'Barter system' (trading materials in exchange for materials, instead of a constant 'currency'), but this time, instead of trading random materials, we will be trading Weights, Biases, Optimizations, Skills, 'Mindsets', 'Mental models', etc. of the AI 'Extensions'. people might stop investing in assets as we know it. there might be accumulations (of knowledge and experience) instead of investments. More importantly , not a lot of things that are currently bought as 'commodities' or 'services', will remain 'commodities' or 'services' for long. For example, Accounting and legal services are examples of services done for human clients by human accountants and lawyers. this by nature, is a very mundane, boring, error-prone way to do your accounting and legal works. It also has a very horrible UX (especially in third-world countries like India). AI will completely remove such a concept. Humans-as-a-service for ANY Niche, will no more be a thing , which is very good for the client or end-user. Same goes for commodities and manufacturing. Currently, vehicles and Electronic appliances attribute a large sum of their prices to labor costs that the manufacturers have to pay for. These labor costs are for the paying the Supervisors, factory workers, Farmers, miners, assembly-line employees, etc. There will be no more of these labor costs after reliable Lights-out manufacturing (fully autonomous manufacturing) comes into the picture. In few places around the world (like a few countries in Asia), there is already a widespread concept of 'Quick Commerce' which is "Shopping online and getting things delivered within 10 minutes to under an hour". I believe with everyone having their own GPUs, there will emerge a new wave of startups that offer quick-commerce for 'services' and 'labors' like Plumbing, electricians, etc. OR even better, your own home robot can do all the work that would otherwise require experts like plumbers and mechanics! the next obvious win-win of GPUs for everyone will be that there won't be any holidays given to Utilitarian institutions like hospitals, schools, airports, banks, etc. because all these institutions will just literally be many rooms filled with GPUs and NPUs, doing all the work, managing everything.

**4. Social Media:** As of right now, Social media is a nightmare to anyone with a conscience. It doesn't need much explanation. I believe with personal AI and personal GPUs for everybody, i envision this as the new 'social media': first and foremost, media will be linked with your education AND overall interests. Media will be generated on-demand. Media will NOT be boring , but will be INTERACTIVE. People won't communicate other people over this 'social media platform'; a person's agent will communicate with that person's friend's agent and only the gists will be passed on to the person. We are already going to see this happen soon with emails , as cold-emails as we know it, won't cease to exist anymore. the recipient's agent will check which emails are worth the human's time and degrade the rest as 'spam'.

**5. Interfaces:** why will a phone exist, when all of its utility happens in your BCI device (like a headband), which is connected to your own GPU? Brain Communication Interface devices will be THE most prominent device in the GPUs-for-everybody future. you think of something, your agent knows about it. you and your agent share the same memory, same preferences, same biases, same opinions, same worldviews, same thoughts, same EVERYTHING. the only mere difference is that one is in the physical world , while one lives digitally in a GPU. Your personal AI though it lives in your GPU, it has many form factors: it can live inside your robot, or your smart home, or your Electric vehicle, all synced with one 'personal AI' that you OWN, literally.

**6. Transport:** the fundamental goal of transport is to move object X from point A to point B, with some nuances of course. When you have your own GPU, your agent (which is connected to your smart home, smart car, and your city's smart cabs-network) will have looked at the traffic, the prices, your calendar, your friend's or family member's calendars, whether your EV is charged up or not, the nearest Cab in the vicinity near you at that moment, etc. You won't have to lift a finger or stress about anything. Your tickets, your bookings, your flight/train delays, everything will be handled. You just arrive at the station or the cab arrives at your pickup. it's all in a state of flow. your agent can communicate with the train driver agent, the flight pilot agent, the cab driver agent, and so on.

**7. Privacy:** Privacy won't be a matter of concern anymore. Your Agent builds your own versions of utility and personal software and you run them on your personal GPU. Now, Since AI model architectures will get drastically better over time, very soon, the GPU 'capacity' needed by your personal AI (in reality, a Fable/Sol level model) will be equivalent to the compute and space needed by the human brain (or at least close enough). this translates to the benefit of being able to use the rest of your GPU to host your own Software and cloud services on your GPU!

**8. Agent-to-Agent Communication Scalability:** As mentioned in point 6, your agent can communicate with Agents running systems. For example, if your train is running late, and while on your way to the railway station you (your agent, actually) find out that there is an alternative route which the train driver (train driver agent) can take , which will allow it to arrive at the station much faster, then your agent can converse directly with the train driver agent and inform him about this. when humans were in charge of everything this wasn't physically possible in any way whatsoever because of scalability issues (how can a single train driver human manage communicating with several passengers personally, all while driving the train?!)
